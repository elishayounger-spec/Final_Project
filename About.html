Our Vision.

I’ve always had a love for cars, almost every toy I had as a child was related to cars. Around middle school I had a small desire to design cars but I couldn’t put my ideas on paper, so for the next few years the desire to create faded. In my eighth grade year my mother was in the market for a new minivan, but no van on the market fitted her wants or needs. So she came to me with the idea to design a van for her. So now I’m here, in the beginning stages of designing a van for my mom and families like mine across the world.
Our vision here is to create a line of electric vehicles that meet the needs and wants of the general public. Being based in California we have the highest percentage of electric cars in the U.S. but not many electric minivans. We will start by creating an electric minivan and then expand to making every other type of car.

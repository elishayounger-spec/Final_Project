ESB Mobility.

Why Choose ESB Mobility

Sustainability.

We focus on building 100% electric viechles that protect the environment. We use clean rescources and energy to produce and manufacture parts and cars.

Community.

Instead of comming up with car ideas withing the company we look to the community and directly ask them what they want. This helps us know exactly how to design our cars so they meet the comsumers wants and needs.

Affordability.

We know that the prices of cars are constantly rising. And that EV's are taking over. That is why we plan to keep the prices of our cars at a resonable rate. We provide the most "bang for your buck" when it comes to the features of the car relative to the price.

Our Focus & Plan.

College Student Involvment.

We plan to start of this company with the development of an electric minivan. We know that there are very few of them in the U.S. market right now and that is an challenge and opportunity we will take. With our moral of being centered on community we will get nearby college students to go out into the community and survey people on the features they would want in the electric minivan. Then they will design the renderings and first stages of the minivan.
